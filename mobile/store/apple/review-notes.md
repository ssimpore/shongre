# App Review notes template

Shongre is a multi-market classifieds app for browsing, publishing physical-goods listings, and communicating with sellers. The candidate does not sell digital promotion/subscription products in the iOS UI. The app is API-only in every environment; review uses the isolated staging API described in the secure App Store Connect notes and cannot fall back to local sample data.

Provide securely at submission:

- reviewer username/password and any verification bypass;
- current staging/API availability and supported market;
- seeded listing/conversation identifiers for report/block testing;
- why selected photos and notifications are requested;
- account deletion path: Account → Settings → Delete account;
- disposable account instructions if deletion must be completed;
- any KYC, order, payment, or market gate and how to exercise it;
- support contact and outage escalation.

Do not paste secrets into this file. Have an unaffiliated tester complete the exact notes on a clean device before submission.
