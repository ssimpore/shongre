# App Store checklist

- [ ] Re-check App Review Guidelines, Upcoming Requirements, agreements, banking, and tax
- [ ] Build with Xcode 26+ and iOS 26 SDK+ (or newer current minimum)
- [ ] Confirm `com.shongre.marketplace` ownership, version/build, distribution signing, and provisioning
- [ ] Inspect signed archive entitlements, privacy manifests, embedded SDK signatures, and required-reason APIs
- [ ] Reconcile App Privacy answers with `privacy-label-map.md` and production processors
- [ ] Test account deletion, report/block, reviewer access, and every permission denial path
- [ ] Approve digital-goods billing path; do not enable unreviewed web steering
- [ ] Verify Universal Links and public AASA response, content type, identifiers, and paths
- [ ] Complete age rating, export compliance, content rights, support/privacy URLs, metadata, and screenshots
- [ ] Submit candidate explicitly; choose phased/manual release separately

This checklist does not predict approval. Record reviewer feedback and update it when the product or policies change.
