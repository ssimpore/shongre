# Google Play App Access

Mark the app as having restricted areas and provide the dedicated reviewer account in Play Console’s secure App Access field. Use the scenarios in `../common/reviewer-access.md`. Credentials must not expire, require an internal VPN, depend on a personal phone, or be committed here.

Document any OTP bypass, KYC sandbox, geographic restriction, active-order deletion guard, feature flag, or payment sandbox. Include a support contact that can respond during review. Validate the instructions on a clean device outside the company network immediately before submission.
