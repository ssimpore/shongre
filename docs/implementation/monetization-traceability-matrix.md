# Monetization requirement traceability matrix

## Scope and evidence rules

This matrix audits the monetization objective supplied on 2026-08-31 against
the repository's canonical catalog, contracts, migrations, backend services,
Web and mobile adapters, and automated tests. A catalog declaration is counted
as complete only when an enforcing consumer and verification evidence exist.

The canonical sources are:

- `AGENTS.md`, especially client boundaries, market isolation, money, provider,
  entitlement, mobile-store and commercial-governance invariants;
- `packages/contracts/src/schemas/monetization.ts` and the active and proposed
  catalog fixtures;
- `backend/openapi/openapi.json`;
- `backend/docs/monetization-platform.md`,
  `docs/architecture/business-rules-monetization.md`, and
  `docs/implementation/monetization-migration-map.md`;
- migrations `00015`, `00024`–`00026`, `00085`, and `00087`;
- the implementation and tests cited below.

## Explicit release blockers

| Blocker                                                                       | Required owner                             | Exact impact                                                                                  | Fail-closed behavior                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Active v3 has no synchronized runtime Stripe price mappings                   | Payments + Finance                         | Database-mode subscription and add-on checkout cannot start                                   | Reject before provider call; demo remains deterministic                 |
| Provider plan-change strategy is `not_configured`                             | Payments + Product                         | Existing live provider subscriptions cannot upgrade/downgrade through the local mutation path | Reject with `PROVIDER_PLAN_CHANGE_NOT_CONFIGURED`; do not change rights |
| FR registry readiness lacks `taxes.mode` evidence                             | Tax/Legal + Market Operations              | No new catalog version, including v4, may be submitted or published                           | Keep v3 historical active snapshot; block submission                    |
| BE/CH commercial and provider data absent                                     | Market owners + Finance + Payments + Legal | Active country experiences have no purchasable Shongre plans                                  | Return unavailable; never copy EUR/CHF prices or France policy          |
| Grace-period rule not approved                                                | Product + Finance + Legal                  | No positive dunning grace is offered                                                          | Suspend paid access immediately on `past_due`                           |
| v4 shadow quotes, campaign bounds, economics and provider evidence incomplete | Product + Finance + Payments + Operations  | Starter/Growth/Performance is preview-only                                                    | `checkoutEnabled=false`; publication validator blocks                   |
| Mobile store billing route not approved                                       | Product + Legal + Mobile Payments          | No mobile digital purchase/change/restore/refund/cancel                                       | Read-only billing; no link, steering or selected rail                   |
| Multi-market legal invoice facts not approved                                 | Legal + Finance                            | HTML billing document cannot be represented as legally final outside configured issuer facts  | No non-FR commercial publication or invoice generation                  |

No blocker above is represented by a fake success, placeholder provider id,
France fallback, or automatically copied market configuration.
